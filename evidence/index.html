What appears here?

Only retained comparison cases appear in this catalog. Planned or not-assessed Quanty capabilities belong in the Test Campaign view and are not presented here as evidence.

This catalog is a static projection of a historical retained dual-platform run. Its hashes and imported run status are not independently re-attested by this Pages PR; the catalog therefore does not itself grant current QES, full Quanty parity, production maturity or material validation.
