QES is a separate quality axis

Quality Evidence Score evaluates implementation, science, tests, engineering and provenance for a bounded capability. It never substitutes for the capability colour and never implies Quanty parity or material validation. Numeric scores are published only from accepted canonical QES records.

Publication boundary

This public view excludes raw evidence payloads, internal filesystem/NAS paths, runner identities, secrets and private execution details. Public numbers are sanitized summaries. Exact internal evidence remains subject to repository CI/review and provenance controls.

Material validation: numerical/reference acceptance is not experimental-sample validation. No material-validation claim is inferred from this dashboard.
